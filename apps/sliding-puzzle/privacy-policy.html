This Privacy Policy explains how CCK Studio (operated by Celal Can Kaya, "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Sliding Puzzle mobile app (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically generated identifier. The App signs you in anonymously the first time you open it, using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random, device-scoped identifier with no email, password, or account registration required.

Information you provide. You may choose a nickname (free text) and an avatar from a built-in set of images. Your nickname is shown publicly on the leaderboard and your profile - please don't enter your real name or other personal information in it.

Gameplay data. Your best completion times, move counts, and games played/solved per board size are saved to our servers under your anonymous identifier, so your personal bests and leaderboard rank can be kept in sync.

Leaderboard. Your best time and move count for each board size may be shown publicly to other players, displayed together with the nickname and avatar you chose.

Diagnostics & crash data. We use Firebase Crashlytics, which automatically collects crash reports, device model, OS version, and app version to help us find and fix bugs.

Usage data. The App uses Firebase Analytics. We do not currently send it any custom events ourselves, but the SDK may automatically collect basic usage information (such as app opens) and associated device identifiers, per Google's standard behavior for that SDK.

Advertising data. The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where applicable law requires your consent before personal data is used to personalize ads (for example, under the GDPR for users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland), we will only enable personalized ads after obtaining that consent through an appropriate consent mechanism; otherwise, you will be shown non-personalized ads.

Support requests. If you contact us (for example via the in-app "Contact & Support" link or by emailing us directly), we receive your email address, the content of your message, and - if sent from the in-app link - a short line of app version and device information that is added to the email draft automatically before you send it.

Locally-stored preferences. Settings such as language, theme, sound/vibration toggles, and whether you've already seen the in-app tutorial are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core gameplay features: save your progress and best scores, and power the leaderboard.
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
  • Display ads, and measure or personalize them where legally permitted.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers who process it on our behalf, strictly to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Remote Config, Crashlytics, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy & Security, How Google uses information from partner sites/apps.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country's law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at cckstudio8@gmail.com.

5. Your Rights

The App has no traditional login, so your data is tied to the anonymous identifier stored on your device. Uninstalling the App stops all local data collection; server-side records tied to your anonymous identifier may still exist until you ask us to delete them. To exercise any of the rights below, contact cckstudio8@gmail.com.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR/UK GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. We rely on consent, our legitimate interests (e.g. crash diagnostics, the leaderboard), and contract performance as our legal bases. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information, and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond what is described in this policy.

If you are in Türkiye (KVKK - Law No. 6698): as data controller based in Türkiye, we process personal data in line with KVKK. Under Article 11, you may learn whether we process your data, request information about it, request correction or deletion, and object to results produced solely by automated analysis.

Anywhere else: we aim to honor equivalent rights for all users regardless of location - just get in touch.

6. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Google/Firebase/AdMob) may process and store data in the United States and other countries where they operate data centers, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Gameplay and profile data tied to your anonymous identifier is retained for as long as needed to provide the leaderboard and stats features, or until you ask us to delete it. Crash reports and diagnostic logs are retained per Firebase's standard retention periods.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Do Not Track

Browsers and devices don't consistently support "Do Not Track" signals, so we don't currently respond to them differently from other requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and, for material changes, provide additional notice (such as an in-app notice) where appropriate.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at cckstudio8@gmail.com.

1. Topladığımız Bilgiler

Otomatik oluşturulan kimlik. Uygulamayı ilk açtığınızda, Firebase Authentication kullanılarak anonim olarak oturum açılır. Bu, e-posta, şifre veya hesap kaydı gerektirmeyen, rastgele ve cihaza özel bir kimlik oluşturur.

Sizin sağladığınız bilgiler. Serbest metin olarak bir takma ad ve hazır görsellerden oluşan bir avatar seçebilirsiniz. Takma adınız liderlik tablosunda ve profilinizde herkese açık şekilde gösterilir - lütfen gerçek adınızı veya başka kişisel bilgilerinizi buraya yazmayın.

Oyun verileri. Her tahta boyutu için en iyi tamamlama sürenizin, hamle sayınızın ve oynanan/çözülen oyun sayınızın, kişisel rekorlarınızın ve liderlik tablosu sıralamanızın senkronize kalabilmesi için anonim kimliğiniz altında sunucularımıza kaydedilir.

Liderlik tablosu. Her tahta boyutu için en iyi süreniz ve hamle sayınız, seçtiğiniz takma ad ve avatarla birlikte diğer oyunculara herkese açık şekilde gösterilebilir.

Tanılama ve çökme verileri. Hataları bulup düzeltmemize yardımcı olmak için, çökme raporlarını, cihaz modelini, işletim sistemi sürümünü ve uygulama sürümünü otomatik olarak toplayan Firebase Crashlytics'i kullanıyoruz.

Kullanım verileri. Uygulama, Firebase Analytics kullanmaktadır. Şu an kendimiz herhangi bir özel etkinlik göndermiyoruz; ancak bu SDK, Google'ın söz konusu SDK için standart davranışı gereği temel kullanım bilgilerini (uygulama açılışları gibi) ve ilişkili cihaz kimliklerini otomatik olarak toplayabilir.

Reklam verileri. Uygulama, Google AdMob tarafından sunulan reklamları gösterir. AdMob ve Google, reklamları sunmak ve ölçmek için cihaz ve reklam kimliklerini toplayabilir. Yürürlükteki mevzuatın kişisel verilerin reklam kişiselleştirmesinde kullanılmadan önce onayınızı gerektirdiği durumlarda (örneğin AB Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık veya İsviçre'deki kullanıcılar için GDPR kapsamında), kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları yalnızca uygun bir onay mekanizmasıyla onayınızı aldıktan sonra etkinleştireceğiz; aksi halde size kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar gösterilir.

Destek talepleri. Bizimle iletişime geçerseniz (uygulama içi "İletişim ve Destek" bağlantısı ile veya doğrudan e-posta yoluyla), e-posta adresinizi, mesajınızın içeriğini ve - uygulama içi bağlantı üzerinden gönderildiyse - e-posta taslağına gönderilmeden önce otomatik olarak eklenen kısa bir uygulama sürümü/cihaz bilgisi satırını alırız.

Cihazda saklanan tercihler. Dil, tema, ses/titreşim ayarları ve uygulama içi öğreticiyi daha önce görüp görmediğiniz gibi ayarlar yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır ve bize hiçbir zaman iletilmez.
